Why Vindauga?

The name means “wind-eye.” The entire identity begins with the window.

Vindauga is rooted in the Old Norse word for window — literally, the “wind-eye.”

A window is where light, landscape, protection, comfort, and craftsmanship meet. The mark turns that idea into something rugged, precise, memorable, and connected to the outdoors.

The Eye Vision, awareness, precision, and seeing the finished result clearly.
The Window The literal wind-eye and the core of every service Vindauga provides.
The Landscape The view beyond the glass — what clean, protected windows are meant to reveal.
The Nordic Detail Strength, craftsmanship, heritage, and a distinct identity that does not blend into the category.
